Questions

Frequently asked

Is Motiq really free?

Yes for athletes, always. During the beta it’s free for coaches and teams too. Team plans start after the beta.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Motiq runs in any phone or computer browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app, and it keeps working without signal.

Is my video private?

The form checker runs on your phone, so your video stays there unless you choose to share it with your coach. We never sell data or show ads.

What sports does it work for?

More than 50 sports and activities, with position-specific programs for team sports like football, soccer, basketball, baseball and volleyball.

How accurate is the form checker?

It tracks 33 body points at up to 60 frames a second and shows how sure it is about every check. A quick camera check before you record makes sure the angle and lighting are right.

Who can use Motiq?

Anyone 13 or older can make their own account. Younger athletes can use Motiq through an account a parent sets up, and under-18s need a parent’s OK before a coach sees their health details.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can download or delete everything at any time from Settings. Read the privacy policy.
