Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-04

The short version
  • Motiq keeps your training, wellness and form-check data so it can build your program and show your coach how you’re doing. That’s the only reason we have it.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Form-check videos are analysed on your phone. A video only leaves your phone if you choose to share it with your coach.
  • Your coach sees your team data. If you’re under 18, a parent or guardian has to approve before your coach can see your health and training details.
  • You can download everything or delete your account at any time in Settings › Privacy and data.
  • Motiq accounts are for people 13 and older. Younger athletes can only use Motiq through an account a parent or guardian sets up and manages.

1. Who we are

Motiq (“Motiq”, “we”, “us”) is a training app for athletes and coaches, available at the address you are reading this on and as an installable web app. Questions about this policy or your data: waskey@toptierwins.com.

3. How we use it

Only to run Motiq for you and your team: building and adjusting programs, scoring form checks, showing progress and alerts, syncing between devices, sending you the messages and announcements your coach posts, keeping the service secure and fixing problems. We don’t use your data to train AI models for anyone else, and we don’t make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who can see it

  • You. Everything you log.
  • Your coaches and team staff, if you join a team. They see your training, check-ins and the form checks you share. Notes you mark private stay private.
  • Your parent or guardian, if they’re linked to your account.
  • A school or club administrator, if your coach links the team to one. They see totals per team (number of athletes, check-ins and sessions), never your own data.
  • Service providers who host and run Motiq for us, under contracts that limit them to doing that: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), GitHub (hosting the app’s files), Google (only if you choose Google sign-in) and Stripe (only if you buy a plan).
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

We never sell or rent personal information, and we don’t “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Health information

Check-ins, injuries, pain and similar details are health information. We ask for your consent before you start, use it only to adjust training and keep you safe, show it only to the people in section 4, and you can delete any entry or your whole account at any time. Motiq is not a medical service and isn’t covered by HIPAA; nothing in the app is a diagnosis.

6. Children and teenagers

  • Motiq accounts are for people 13 and older. We don’t knowingly let children under 13 create their own account.
  • A child under 13 may use Motiq only through an account a parent or guardian creates and manages. If we learn that a child under 13 created an account without that, we delete it.
  • For athletes under 18 on a team, a parent or guardian must approve (with their own Motiq parent account) before the coach can see the athlete’s profile, check-ins, workouts, videos or injuries.
  • Parents can ask to see or delete their child’s information at any time using the contact above.

7. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account we delete your cloud data straight away; backups held by our database provider roll off within 30 days. Error reports are kept for up to 90 days. Data stored only on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and download everything: Settings › Privacy and data › Download my data.
  • Correct anything by editing it in the app.
  • Delete single entries in the app, or your whole account: Settings › Privacy and data › Delete my account.
  • Stop sharing with a coach by leaving the team.
  • Turn off error reports in Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU and UK), you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict processing or to complain to a data protection authority. We honour these requests for everyone; contact us and we’ll respond within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for using them. Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for health information and on providing the service you asked for for everything else.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our database provider, which encrypts it at rest. Database rules check, on the server, who is allowed to read each record. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we’ll tell you as the law requires.

10. Where data is stored

Our providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, we use standard contractual protections for these transfers.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll show the new version in the app and ask you to accept it before you continue. The version and date are at the top of this page.
